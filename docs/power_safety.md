# Electrical Power Safety & Current Limiter Architecture                                                                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    
  Driving arrays of addressable RGB LEDs presents critical power constraints. This document details the mathematical model and real-time execution of the **Active Current Limiter**.                                                             
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  
  ---                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  
  ## 1. Electrical Hazard Analysis                                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  
  - **WS2812B/WS2815 Power Profile:** Each pixel consists of internal IC logic and 3 distinct LED dies (Red, Green, Blue).                                                                                                                        
  - **Peak Current:** When displaying full white ($R=255, G=255, B=255$) at 100% brightness, a single pixel draws up to **~55 - 60 mA**.                                                                                                          
  - **Hazard:** Driving 100 LEDs at peak white demands $> 5.5 \text{ A}$. Without regulation, this leads to voltage sag, PCB trace overheating, supply brownouts, and thermal degradation.                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  
  ---                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  
  ## 2. Mathematical Current Estimation Model                                                                                                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  
  The projected current consumption $I_{\text{frame}}$ is evaluated for every frame buffer prior to RMT hardware transmission:                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  
  $$I_{\text{frame}} = I_{\text{quiescent}} + \sum_{i=0}^{N-1} \left( k_r \cdot R_i + k_g \cdot G_i + k_b \cdot B_i \right)$$                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  
  Where:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  - $N$: Total number of active LEDs.                                                                                                                                                                                                             
  - $I_{\text{quiescent}}$: Standby current consumed by internal pixel driver ICs ($\approx 1.0 \text{ mA}$ per LED).                                                                                                                             
  - $k_r, k_g, k_b$: Empirical current coefficients per 8-bit channel value:                                                                                                                                                                      
    - $k_r = 0.21 \text{ mA / unit}$ ($\approx 18 \text{ mA}$ max per Red die)                                                                                                                                                                    
    - $k_g = 0.15 \text{ mA / unit}$ ($\approx 13 \text{ mA}$ max per Green die)                                                                                                                                                                  
    - $k_b = 0.15 \text{ mA / unit}$ ($\approx 13 \text{ mA}$ max per Blue die)                                                                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  
  ---                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  
  ## 3. Dynamic Scaling Factor Calculation                                                                                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  
  If the projected current exceeds the hardware thermal limit ($I_{\text{budget}} = 2000 \text{ mA}$), a dynamic scale factor $S$ is applied uniformly across the entire frame:                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  
  $$S = \begin{cases}                                                                                                                                                                                                                             
  1.0 & \text{if } I_{\text{frame}} \le I_{\text{budget}} \\                                                                                                                                                                                      
  \dfrac{I_{\text{budget}} - I_{\text{quiescent}}}{I_{\text{frame}} - I_{\text{quiescent}}} & \text{if } I_{\text{frame}} > I_{\text{budget}}                                                                                                     
  \end{cases}$$                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  
  ### Color Preservation                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  By scaling all subpixels ($R, G, B$) by the scalar factor $S$, **perceived color hue and saturation remain completely preserved**, dynamically attenuating luminance to guarantee total power stays strictly within the 2000 mA safe operating  
  zone.
